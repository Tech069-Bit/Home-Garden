Privacy Policy

My Garden · Version 1.0

Your data stays on your device

Plants, photos, ideas, fertiliser records and settings are stored locally on your device (in your browser's storage), not on a My Garden server. We don't see it, sell it, or share it with advertisers.

Optional features that do send data

A few features are opt-in and only run if you turn them on:

  • AI plant lookups — if you add your own Gemini API key, the plant name or species you're viewing is sent to Google's Gemini API to fetch care facts.
  • Weather-aware reminders — if enabled, your approximate location is sent to a weather provider to check conditions.
  • Account / Pro plan — if you choose to log in, your email is used to identify your account and plan.
  • Connected folder / backup export — any backup file you create is saved only to the destination you pick (a folder on your device, or wherever you choose to share it). We never receive a copy.

No ads, no tracking

My Garden doesn't run ads and doesn't use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Your control

You can export, share, or permanently erase your data at any time from Settings → Storage.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer, Dr.Vicky.
